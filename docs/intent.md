# Intent

Stated by the author on 2026-11-03. Every later decision is checked against this file; change it here, not in the ADRs.

## What this is
A public model of how to model liberation: which rules hold an institution together, what a change forces or permits, which change to make first. It is both a theory to test and a tool to use ("this is everything"). Every result ships as a plain sentence plus the formal certificate behind it; no plain sentence without one.

## Who it is for
Anyone who wants to check the work. It must be peer-reviewable: public repo from the start, reproducible runs, expectations written down before runs, a methods write-up as the first public document.

## What counts as liberation
Freedom (exit, voice, recognition, non-domination) plus flourishing (pleasure, meaning, being known). Scoring is additive and modular: each can run alone or summed, and disagreement between them is reported, not resolved.

## How change happens
Unknown. Material conditions, coordination, and imagination are all live hypotheses. The model runs them as switchable modules, alone or together, and backtests report which mechanism each historical case needed.

## Rightness and legibility
Both. The cost is scope and two layers of documentation: a small, tested formal core and a plain interpretation guide.

## Openness
Public repository, permissive license with attribution (Apache-2.0 unless changed). Dual-use limits per ADR 0011.

## The author's own arrangements
May be encoded as test institutions in a private `me/` family (philosophy-me), never in the public repo.

## Pace
As fast as possible. Prefer finishing a thin end-to-end path over opening new fronts.

## What would convince the author the frame is wrong (stated 2026-11-03)
- **Kill switch for the computational part:** after honest encoding (power derived from rules, breakers for every obligation, precommitted abstraction levels), the engine still cannot rediscover known findings: the kibbutz regendering via its implicit rule, police vs crisis team, cosmetic reform. High signal.
- **Would surprise the author, and would demote the theory from planner to description:** rules turn out to trail change rather than lead it: technology, demography or prices shift first and rules follow (formula and the pill before leave policy; kerosene before whaling law). Backtests must be chosen to test the ordering, not assume it.
- **Plausible, so tested as hypotheses rather than defended:** floor-first may be one path among several (property acts and no-fault divorce gave standing before any floor); displacement may be the rule rather than a failure mode; the three mechanisms may not separate; freedom and flourishing may conflict more than add; the bundle may not be the unit; the abstraction level may be a dial, not a fact (ADR 0010 is the guard).
