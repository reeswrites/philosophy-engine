# 0005. Isomorphism as the headline result

## Context
The result we care about is that two institutions share a form, for example police, child protective services and the coverture household as care plus control. Such matches cross the line between human and non-human subjects, where a careless "same" is both false and harmful.

## Decision
Isomorphism is established by canonical rule skeleton plus Z3 equivalence under a role and predicate bijection. Every iso report lists the matched sub-signature and the unmatched rules. A bare "iso: true" across the human/non-human line fails the gate. Isomorphism is structural only; it never asserts moral equivalence.

## Alternatives rejected
A similarity score: hides what matched. Whole-institution equivalence only: almost nothing matches, and the interesting partial overlaps are lost.

## Consequences
Reports are longer, and the unmatched list is as informative as the match. Minimal pairs make the remainder visible, for example a guardianship that differs from ownership by one rule. Downstream text must not turn a structural match into a claim about worth or status.

## Status
accepted
