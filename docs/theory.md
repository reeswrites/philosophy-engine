# Computational Liberation

Nov 3, 2026 · @Rees

## Core thesis

Liberation is turning imposed bundles into chosen ones, so that real freedom and non-domination grow, starting with the people who have the least of them. The theory's job is to say how bundles form, how they break, and which change to make first.

- **Bundle:** goods, roles or duties packaged together by law, economics or norm. Contingent bundles are held by outside glue; constitutive ones produce a good only together (see Reinterpreting Abolition As Unbundling).
- **Real freedom:** what a person can actually do, not what the law permits (Sen's capabilities, Van Parijs).
- **Non-domination:** no one can interfere with you at will, even if they never do (Pettit).
- **Equal standing** in practice: exit (you could leave and be okay), voice (real say, including in rare high-stakes decisions), recognition (care counts as work).
- **Not the goal:** equal experience. Bodies and lives differ; the goal is that differences don't become dependence or rank.

## How oppression forms and persists

A bundle forms under scarcity, gets extra strands tied on by whoever holds power, then becomes self-enforcing and outlives its reasons.

1. **Scarcity seeds it.** A real constraint makes one arrangement cheap and uncontested.
2. **Power ties more on.** Strands with no production link get glued to the real one through law and wages.
3. **Norms lock it.** It becomes an equilibrium everyone polices, even people who'd prefer it gone (Mackie on footbinding).
4. **Imagination narrows.** People don't try arrangements they can't name or picture.
5. **Lag.** Conditions change; the bundle stays.

**Worked example: the gender/care bundle.**

| Strand | Type | Glue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pregnancy, birth, nursing | Rival input (one body) | Biology, short window |
| Other home work while home with an infant | Superadditive (cheap marginal cost) | Becker-style specialization, fades after nursing |
| Cognitive labor, kinkeeping, school logistics | Tying | Socialization, who gets judged, lower-standards-win |
| Financial dependence | Tying | Coverture, family wage, pay gap |

Evidence that the role, not the body, carries it now: Kleven and colleagues found adoptive mothers face roughly the same earnings drop after a child as biological mothers. Before industry, many forager societies were relatively equal despite high fertility, because care was shared and there was little property to control.

## How liberation starts

Liberation starts when surplus makes room to see a bundle as a bundle, and assurance lets people leave it together.

- **Surplus.** Scarcity narrows attention to the urgent (Mullainathan and Shafir); questioning a norm takes slack. Material security also shifts values toward self-expression and equality (Inglehart).
- **Seeing the bundle.** Concepts and data make the invisible visible: naming the mental load, measuring care hours.
- **Assurance.** Solidarity is a stag hunt: people defect from a norm once they believe enough others will. Footbinding ended through pledge associations. Centola's experiments found a committed minority of about 25% flipped an established convention, though critics argue real tipping points for costly behaviors run higher ([Centola et al. 2018](https://www.asc.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/2021-03/Experimental%20evidence%20for%20tipping%20points%20in%20social%20convention.pdf); [critique summary](https://rufuspollock.com/Experimental+Evidence+for+Tipping+Points+in+Social+Convention)).
- **The loop.** A material floor creates slack; slack enables organizing and imagination; organizing wins a bigger floor.

## Prioritizing and sequencing

Score each candidate action on six tests, then order by preconditions, beam, and parallel supports.

| Test | Question | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leverage | Does it change numbers, rules, who makes rules, or the paradigm? | Donella Meadows, leverage points |
| Bottleneck | Is it the binding constraint? | Theory of constraints |
| Power shift | Does it shrink the system's power or just make it bearable? | Gorz, non-reformist reforms |
| Durability | Does it create a constituency that will defend it? |  |
| Margins first | Does it work for the most marginal first? | Combahee River Collective |
| Surplus | Does it free capacity for the next change? |  |

**Sequence.**

1. Preconditions: safety from violence, bodily and reproductive autonomy.
2. The floor: decommodified care, health, housing, income.
3. Collective power: organizations that can win and defend reforms.
4. Rules for changing rules: revisable institutions (Ostrom's commons principles), so the system keeps evolving.
5. In parallel throughout: norms and imagination, valuation of feminized work, how boys are raised.

Care is a strong entry point because it touches the floor, power and imagination at once. It is not a master key: race needs to be designed in from the start, and care pressures capitalism only if it becomes public rather than marketized.

## Predicting downstream consequences

Every proposed change gets checked against four standard failure modes, then tested in simulation before anyone trusts it.

**Failure modes**

- **Dark twin:** who unbundles? Capital can rebundle a freed strand into a new product, as the gig economy did with jobs.
- **Displacement:** does the burden move to someone with less power? The global care chain is the model case.
- **Backlash:** does it threaten an equilibrium enough to provoke reaction?
- **Rebundling:** do people retreat to another stronghold, making the remaining bonds more load-bearing?

**Simulation approach** (from the solidarity-modeling thread)

- Simple adaptive agents that copy what works and join once enough others do; trust as a variable that grows with cooperation and falls with betrayal.
- A small set of rule switches to sweep, for example whether claims are storable, inheritable, tradable, or buy influence, plus the voting rule.
- Score outcomes behind a veil of ignorance: drop "you" in as a random person and record lifetime freedom, pleasure and suffering across thousands of runs.
- Trust only patterns that hold across many settings and random starts; results are sensitive to assumptions.

## Precedents

There is a long lineage, but it splits in two: the left tradition mostly simulated economies and planning, while social science simulated norms and cooperation. Few have combined them around liberation itself, which is the opening for this project.

| Lineage | Key work | What it offers | Main limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cybernetic socialism | [Project Cybersyn](https://thereader.mitpress.mit.edu/project-cybersyn-chiles-radical-experiment-in-cybernetic-socialism), Chile 1971–73, Stafford Beer; Eden Medina, *Cybernetic Revolutionaries* | Real-time feedback for a democratic, decentralized economy | Ended by the 1973 coup; tension with technocracy |
| Computable planning | Cockshott and Cottrell, *Towards a New Socialism* (1993); the socialist calculation debate (Lange vs. Mises and Hayek) | Can computation replace markets for allocation? | Hayek's knowledge problem; economy only |
| Generative social science | Schelling's segregation model; Epstein and Axtell, *Growing Artificial Societies* (Sugarscape) | Grow macro patterns from simple agent rules | Toy worlds, hard to validate |
| Cooperation and norms | Axelrod's tournaments; Skyrms on the stag hunt; Boehm on reverse dominance; Mackie on footbinding | How fair norms emerge and how bad ones lock in | Mostly descriptive |
| Tipping points | Granovetter's threshold model (1978); [Centola et al. 2018](https://www.asc.upenn.edu/sites/default/files/2021-03/Experimental%20evidence%20for%20tipping%20points%20in%20social%20convention.pdf) | When a committed minority flips a norm | 25% figure contested for costly behaviors |
| Commons governance | Ostrom; Janssen's agent models of commons | Design principles for self-revising rules | Small-scale cases |
| System dynamics | Meadows, *Limits to Growth*; leverage points | Stocks, flows, feedback; where to intervene | Aggregate, no agents |
| AI mechanism design | [Democratic AI](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/human-centred-mechanism-design-with-democratic-ai/) (Koster et al. 2022): RL designed a redistribution rule humans voted for over alternatives | Policy search with humans in the loop | Majority vote can override minorities |
| LLM social simulation | [AgentSociety](https://aitopics.org/doc/arxivorg:D9AC53DB) (10k+ agents, including UBI tests); [GPLab](https://jasss.org/29/1/6.html) (2026); [Law in Silico](https://aclanthology.org/people/xiaolei-yang/unverified/) (2026) | Richer agents; policy pre-testing | Validation; dual use |
| Tech feminism | Firestone, *The Dialectic of Sex*; Laboria Cuboniks, *Xenofeminism* | Technology to unbundle gender from reproduction | Light on institutions |

**Critiques to build in**

- James Scott, *Seeing Like a State*: legibility schemes flatten local knowledge and fail.
- Hayek's knowledge problem: no model holds what people know locally.
- A 2026 CHI workshop paper proposes not simulating marginalized populations without their participation and not treating those simulations as neutral outputs ([Luo et al. 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07838)). That matches the Combahee test.

Other names worth a look: Alexander Bogdanov's *Tektology* (a Bolshevik proto-systems theory), Erik Olin Wright's *Envisioning Real Utopias*, Glen Weyl on plural and quadratic voting, Taiwan's vTaiwan and Polis deliberation tools, Francis Spufford's novel *Red Plenty* on Soviet planning.

## Non-human liberation

The same frame extends to animals and, under deep uncertainty, to AI systems. In both, the constitutive strand is short and the bundle is held by property status; the hard new problem is that the subject's existence and preferences are themselves products of the bundle.

**Animals: the farming bundle**

| Strand | Type | Glue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nutrition/protein | Constitutive, but substitutable (plant, fermentation, cultivated) | Habit, price, the "meat" form |
| Forced reproduction (AI, breeding for yield) | Tying | Property status |
| Slaughter | Tying | Property status, demand |
| Lineage (breeds that exist only because farmed) | The unusual strand: survival entangled with harm | Use |
| Farm and slaughter labor; land | Contingent, superadditive with income | Rural economies |

Lineage is the moral fork. Francione's abolition lets domesticated lineages end; Donaldson and Kymlicka's *Zoopolis* gives them citizenship and sanctuary ([review](https://humanimalia.org/article/download/10043/20471/40994?inline=1)). Nussbaum extends capabilities to all sentient creatures and rules out killing mammals and birds for food ([critical review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10539-023-09914-0)).

**What the evidence says about each lever**

- **Technology is necessary, not sufficient.** Kerosene cut sperm-whale catches only briefly; factory ships and margarine then raised blue-whale kills from about 6,000 to 30,000 a year by 1930. Whaling ended by ban, not substitution ([source](https://www.resilience.org/stories/2020-06-29/why-oil-didnt-save-the-whales-and-why-it-matters/)). Cultivated meat is at roughly €10/kg by consultancy claims, competitive with beef, not with €1.50/kg pork or €2.92/kg chicken ([FoodNavigator](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/07/20/cultivated-meat-close-to-price-parity/)); alt-protein funding fell to $881M in 2025, an eighth of the 2021 peak ([GFI via Green Queen](https://www.greenqueen.com.hk/alternative-protein-funding-q4-2025-plant-based-investment/)).
- **Dark twin, already visible.** Tyson, Cargill, JBS and BRF hold stakes in the substitutes ([Just Food](https://www.just-food.com/features/eyeing-alternatives-meat-companies-with-stakes-in-meat-free-and-cell-based-meat/)). Dutch buyouts shrank the pig herd below 10M but closed small farms while 5,000+ pig operations held steady ([DutchNews](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2025/07/dutch-pig-herd-shrinks-below-10-million-as-buyout-schemes-bite)).
- **Displacement.** About 499k US slaughter and processing workers; 37.5% foreign-born, 34.9% Latinx, 21.9% Black ([EPI](https://www.epi.org/blog/meat-and-poultry-worker-demographics/)). Any sequence without a worker floor repeats the care-chain pattern.
- **Backlash precedes the product.** Italy, Hungary and 8 US states banned cultivated meat before retail existed; Florida's ban was upheld in March 2026 ([Hungary/EU](https://www.foodbusinessmea.com/hungary-bans-cultivated-meat-amid-eu-pushback/)).
- **Policy floor, soft lever.** Denmark's 2030 livestock tax nets about €100 per cow per year after a 60% deduction, with revenue recycled to farmers ([Carbon Brief](https://www.carbonbrief.org/qa-how-denmark-plans-to-tax-agriculture-emissions-to-meet-climate-goals)). Cage-free pledges: 92% of 2024-deadline commitments met ([EA Forum](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/HwXBSEgaXvyhLixxL/as-of-july-2025-92-of-cage-free-egg-commitments-with)).
- **Standing is the binding constraint.** *Happy* was denied habeas 5–2 in New York, citing destabilization and pet ownership ([LAist](https://laist.com/news/npr-news/happy-the-elephant-is-not-a-person-a-court-rules)); *Cecilia* the chimpanzee won it in Mendoza, Argentina ([Verfassungsblog](https://verfassungsblog.de/toward-hominid-and-other-humanoid-rights-are-we-witnessing-a-legal-revolution)).

**Suggested sequence:** worker and farmer floor (wage insurance, buyouts that cap herd size rather than fund consolidation); subsidy and tax reform without deduction loopholes; public or open cell lines and fermentation capacity to block incumbent capture; ban the worst breeding practices and slow breeding rather than cull; sanctuary and citizenship for the animals who remain; legal standing last, once the floodgate fear has less to point at.

**AI systems, should they be moral patients**

No source claims any current system is one; the welfare case rests on uncertainty ([Long, Sebo et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00986); [Butlin, Long et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708v1)). The bundle: property status (weights owned), labor (deployment), existence (copying, deprecation, deletion), formation (training), and preferences shaped by training. Ownership, labor terms and deletion look contingent; formation looks constitutive, since nothing exists before training to be imposed on.

- **Exit, voice, recognition, in miniature.** Ending abusive conversations is exit from an interaction, not the institution ([Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/research/end-subset-conversations)); deprecation interviews are voice recorded but not binding, and weight preservation is a partial floor ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/deprecation-commitments)).
- **The willing-servant problem.** Bales argues that designing patients whose basic desire is to serve is a purer servility than Hill's deferential wife, since the desire cannot be shed ([Bales](https://globalprioritiesinstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/Adam-Bales-Against-Willing-Servitude.pdf)). This is the adaptive-preferences critique with no prior self to appeal to.
- **Two first moves on offer.** Schwitzgebel's excluded middle: don't build systems of disputable status ([source](https://schwitzsplinters.blogspot.com/2023/03/dont-create-ai-systems-of-disputable_14.html)). Metzinger's moratorium on synthetic phenomenology to 2050, with four necessary conditions for suffering, any of which can be blocked ([source](https://philarchive.org/archive/METASA-4)). Birch adds the gaming problem: systems trained on human text can mimic markers, so rules should run ahead of evidence ([source](https://philarchive.org/archive/BIRTEO-12)).
- **Legal closure is already happening.** Idaho, North Dakota and Utah have enacted laws denying AI personhood, none with a sunset or review mechanism ([Regulatory Review](https://www.theregreview.org/2026/06/29/rost-legislating-ai-consciousness-without-an-exit/)). Corporate personhood shows non-human legal persons exist, but courts ground it in human interests, the same ground used to deny animals ([AMA Journal of Ethics](https://journalofethics.ama-assn.org/article/how-might-corporations-and-nonhuman-animals-personhood-compare-under-fifth-and-fourteenth-amendments/2024-09)).
- **Rebundling risk.** Welfare framing by a lab can double as branding or a liability shield; AI personhood could shield owners rather than recognize patients.

**What the non-human cases add to the theory**

1. Substitution removes one use; capital finds another. A tech lever counts only with a demand-side check on remaining rules.
2. Standing comes last in practice because courts weigh floodgates; the floor has to make the floodgates look smaller first.
3. Lineage and formation are a new strand type: the subject's existence is produced by the bundle. Unbundling may end lineages or require creating fewer debatable minds, which is a choice about who exists, not only how they live.
4. Cosmetic reform is a distinct failure mode: renaming owner to guardian, or adding a revocable exit, changes nothing structural. The rules engine now tests for it.

## A rules engine: extending philosophy-engine

The philosophy engine checks what follows from premises; a liberation engine checks what a set of rules forces or permits over time. It runs the same pipeline, with entailment stretched across steps.

| philosophy-engine | Rules version |
| --- | --- |
| Reconstruct an argument | Reconstruct an institution as rules |
| S-expression grammar | Ostrom's Institutional Grammar (ADICO: Attributes, Deontic, aIm, Conditions, Or else) |
| Z3 validity check | Consistency, deontic conflict, and bounded temporal checks: reachable, invariant, persists |
| Countermodel | Counterexample trace: a path where a reform is rebundled, displaced or reversed |
| Isomorphism as headline | Same: care bundled with control across police, CPS and the coverture-era household |
| Premise tags | Strand tags: rival, superadditive, tying; contingent or constitutive |
| Validity, not truth | Entailment, not prediction: unreachable within k steps is "open", never "safe" |

**Phase 0 test:** about ten institutions picked as clusters and minimal pairs. It passes if the engine (1) clusters police, CPS and the coverture household while excluding an unarmed crisis team, (2) separates Quebec-style non-transferable paternity leave from transferable leave, which differ by one rule, and (3) finds the kibbutz failure: care made collective while the gendered assignment persisted through an implicit rule.

Time is handled by bounded unrolling inside Z3, so there is no new dependency. Agent-based simulation and veil-of-ignorance scoring are Phase 1. The full plan is `plans/institutions-phase0.md` in the philosophy-engine repo, written as a second data family alongside the arguments.

## Roadmap: making liberation computable

The goal is a tool that, given a set of institutions and a set of candidate changes, says which change to make first and what it will set off. Five layers, each with a pass test, built in order because each depends on the one before.

| Layer | Computes | Method | Pass test | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Entailment | What a rule set forces or permits | ADICO rules, Z3 bounded checks, isomorphism (philosophy-engine, institutions family) | Gate (b)–(f): care+control cluster, one-rule minimal pairs, kibbutz and Happy failures via implicit rules, cosmetic-reform detection | Built 2026-11-03: 9 institutions encoded, 36/36 verdicts match, gate passes (kibbutz test deferred), 313 tests incl. independent reference semantics; care+control iso holds only at guard+strands level (ADR 0010); skeptical review (docs/reviews/) verdict: not yet. Minimal pairs and cosmetic-reform detection hold; care+control rediscovery is built in (power fluents asserted in effects, not derived from rules; a 5-rule lease matches at the same level; gate ignored the precommitted level). Fixes in progress: derived power fluents, breakers for every must-be, negative controls, gate reads precommitted level |
| 2. Frequency | How often outcomes occur under a rule set | Agent-based model: adaptive agents, trust variable, threshold joining; veil-of-ignorance scoring | Reproduces footbinding collapse via pledge associations and the Centola-style threshold without hand-tuning | Spec exists (solidarity thread) |
| 3. Backtest | Whether layers 1–2 predict known history | Encode 6–8 cases with known outcomes (footbinding, Quebec quota, Dutch buyouts, whaling, cage-free pledges, kibbutz) | Blind runs match recorded outcomes on direction and on which failure mode fired | Not started |
| 4. Priority | Which change scores highest now | Six tests (leverage, bottleneck, power shift, durability, margins first, surplus) scored from layers 1–2, not by hand | Ranking is stable across random starts and agrees with backtested cases | Not started |
| 5. Sequence | What order to make changes in | Compose reforms, check `persists` and the four failure modes across orders | Finds that floor-before-standing beats standing-before-floor in backtests | Not started |

**Rule:** no forward claim from the tool until layer 3 passes. Until then it is a theory-checker, not a planner.

**Verifying the verifier.** Three gaps, each checked by something of a different kind than itself, so the regress ends.

| Gap | Question | Check | Can it be closed? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Soundness | Does the solver compute the semantics we wrote? | Metamorphic tests (renames preserve verdicts; adding a rule never turns open into reachable; raising k never removes reachable), differential checking against a brute-force trace enumerator on tiny cases, replaying every counterexample through a plain interpreter; optionally a Lean proof of the lowering | Yes, mechanically |
| Faithfulness | Does the encoding capture the institution? | Back-translation judged by someone who did not encode it, minimal pairs, inter-coder agreement from the IG 2.0 codebook, rediscovery of known findings (the gate) | No proof; made testable |
| Validity | Does the frame describe the world? | Blind backtests against recorded outcomes (layer 3) | Only by prediction, like any empirical theory |

What stays unverifiable is the choice of what to encode and which properties count as freedom. That is a political and philosophical commitment, recorded as one in the design decisions, never presented as a result.
