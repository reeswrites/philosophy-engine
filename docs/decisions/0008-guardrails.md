# 0008. Guardrails

## Context
Institutions govern real people and animals. Agent-based layers invite simulated populations, and the history behind several cases is contested.

## Decision
No simulated populations stand in for real marginalized groups without their participation (see arXiv:2604.07838). Phase 0 has no simulation at all. Contested history goes in notes as rival readings, each attributed to its source, and is not built into the encoding. Where a case is unverified or disputed, the entry says so.

## Alternatives rejected
Simulating groups from published descriptions: replaces the people with the analyst's model of them. Choosing one reading in the encoding: hides the dispute inside rules that look neutral.

## Consequences
Some questions cannot be answered until participatory work exists. Encodings may be thinner than the historiography. Reviewers can see where a disagreement lives, because it sits in notes and not in the rules.

## Status
accepted
